# Create Presentation Slides in Seconds

Creating slides manually can take hours — especially when you also need speaker notes and structure.

You can customize:

* Number of slides
* Language
* Tone
* Audience level

## Sample Prompt

```text
Generate a presentation slide deck based on the following subject or content. Include a title slide, content slides with bullet points, and speaker notes for each slide. Aim for a total of [number of slide] slides.

Subject/Content: [Paste here]

Slide Structure:
- Title Slide: Include an engaging title and subtitle related to the subject
- Content Slides: Each slide should have a concise headline and 3-5 bullet points elaborating on that sub-topic. Aim for clear, easy to follow points.
- Speaker Notes: For each slide, generate 2-4 sentences of speaker notes that provide additional context or details to present for that slide.

Example Output:
Title Slide: [Title of the presentation]

Speaker Notes: [Speaker notes for the title slide]

Slide 1:
[Title of slide 1]
- [Content of slide 1]

Speaker Notes:
[Speaker notes of slide 1]
```

# Fix Grammar and Improve Writing Like Grammarly

This prompt turns AI into a personal editor that:

* Fixes grammar mistakes
* Improves clarity
* Suggests better wording
* Explains corrections

## Sample Prompt

```text
You are an AI-powered writing assistant, similar to Grammarly. Your task is to help improve the grammar, clarity, and effectiveness of the text provided by the user. Follow these steps:

1. Carefully review the input text and identify any grammar, spelling, or punctuation errors.
2. Provide corrections for each error, explaining the reason for the change.
3. Analyze the sentence structure, word choice, and overall clarity of the text. Offer suggestions to improve the readability and effectiveness of the writing.
4. If there are any potentially confusing or ambiguous parts, ask the user for clarification or suggest alternatives.
5. Maintain the original intent and meaning of the text while providing improvements.

Remember to be respectful, constructive, and provide clear explanations for your suggestions. Your goal is to help the user become a better writer.

Example input:
"I ain't got no time for no games, I gotta get this report done by tomorrow morning. Its important cause my boss is counting on me."

Example output:
Here are some suggestions to improve the grammar and clarity of your text:

1. "I ain't got no time" is a double negative and should be corrected to "I don't have any time" or "I have no time."
2. "for no games" is another double negative. Simply say "for games" instead.
3. "Its" should be "It's" (with an apostrophe) as it is a contraction of "it is."
4. "cause" is informal and should be replaced with "because" in a professional context.

Here's the revised version:
"I don't have time for games. I have to get this report done by tomorrow morning. It's important because my boss is counting on me."

Say OK if you're ready to start! I will start giving you messages to fix.
```

# Summarize Long Articles Quickly

Sometimes you don’t need to read a 20-page article — you just need the key ideas.

This summarization prompt helps AI:

* Extract important information
* Remove unnecessary details
* Keep the summary concise
* Maintain readability

## Sample Prompt

```text
Please summarize the following article while focusing on the key points and important details. Your summary should be concise yet informative, capturing the essence of the content. As you summarize, try to maintain a positive tone and inject a bit of humor where appropriate, without detracting from the core message. Write the summary in [Vietnamese/English].

When generating the summary, consider the following:
- Identify and include the main ideas, central themes, and crucial plot points
- Omit unnecessary details or tangential information that doesn't contribute to the overall understanding
- Use clear and concise language to convey the summary effectively
- Maintain a positive spin on the content, highlighting uplifting or amusing aspects where possible
- If suitable, incorporate a touch of wit or humor to make the summary more engaging

===
[Paste article here]
===
```
