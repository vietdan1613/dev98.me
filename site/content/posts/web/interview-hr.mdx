# HR Questions

## 1. Can you introduce yourself?

**Keywords:** `Who you are` · `role` · `core skills`

**Expectations:** Introduce who you are, your current role, and the core skills that match the job.

---

## 2. What do you know about our company?

**Keywords:** `product` · `domain` · `why it interests you`

**Expectations:** Show that you understand the company’s product or domain, then explain why it interests you.

---

## 3. Tell me about a project you’ve been working on

**Keywords:** `Project` · `Role` · `Problem` · `Solution` · `Tech` · `Result`

**Expectations:** Walk through Project → Role → Problem → Solution → Tech → Result so the story stays clear and outcome-focused.

---

## 4. What interests you about this position?

**Keywords:** `role fit` · `skills` · `growth`

**Expectations:** Connect the role to your skills and to the growth you want next.

---

## 5. When you get stuck, how do you proceed?

**Keywords:** `break down` · `research` · `ask for help`

**Expectations:** Explain that you break the problem down and research first, then ask for help with a clear summary of what you tried.

---

## 6. How do you learn and improve your skills?

**Keywords:** `practice` · `documentation` · `people`

**Expectations:** Mention deliberate practice, reading documentation, and learning from teammates as the ways you improve.

---

## 7. How do you handle a challenging feature?

**Keywords:** `requirements` · `split tasks` · `incremental`

**Expectations:** Walk through clarifying the requirements, splitting the work into small tasks, and delivering incrementally.

---

## 8. What tools do you use to manage your work?

**Keywords:** `VS Code` · `Cursor` · `Git` · `Vitest` · `Jira` · `Trello`

**Expectations:** Name your common stack: an editor (VS Code / Cursor), Git, tests (Vitest), and a tracker (Jira / Trello).

---

## 9. How do you plan and organize a new project?

**Keywords:** `requirements` · `plan tasks` · `priorities` · `milestones`

**Expectations:** Walk through clarifying requirements and planning tasks, then setting priorities and milestones so you finish on time.

---

## 10. How do you handle stress and pressure?

**Keywords:** `stay calm` · `prioritize` · `break down`

**Expectations:** Explain that you stay calm, prioritize what matters most, and break large pressure into smaller steps.

---

## 11. What are your strengths?

**Keywords:** `problem-solving` · `responsibility` · `willingness to learn`

**Expectations:** Highlight strengths such as problem-solving, ownership, and willingness to learn.

---

## 12. What are your weaknesses?

**Keywords:** `honest` · `improving` · `English`

**Expectations:** Be honest and name a real area (for example English communication), then show how you are improving it.

---

## 13. What is your preferred working or management style?

**Keywords:** `collaboration` · `feedback` · `teamwork`

**Expectations:** Explain that you prefer collaboration, clear feedback, and working as a team toward shared goals.

---

## 14. Have you mentored juniors? What is your leadership style?

**Keywords:** `Mentor` · `Explain` · `Guide` · `Review` · `Feedback`

**Expectations:** Describe leading by mentoring: you explain, guide, review work, and give actionable feedback.

---

## 15. Where do you see yourself in five years?

**Keywords:** `impact` · `growth` · `engineer`

**Expectations:** Describe your aim to become a more impactful engineer with deeper ownership and stronger technical judgment.

---

## 16. What are your salary expectations?

**Keywords:** `open discussion` · `market` · `role scope`

**Expectations:** Stay open to discussion and frame your expectations around market range and role scope.

---

## 17. Can you describe your CI/CD experience?

**Keywords:** `Git` · `GitHub Actions` · `CI` · `Vercel` · `CD`

**Expectations:** Walk through your typical flow: Git repository → GitHub Actions for CI → Vercel (or similar) for CD.

---

## 18. Walk through your software development process

**Keywords:** `Requirements` · `Planning` · `Design` · `Development` · `Testing` · `Deployment` · `Maintenance`

**Expectations:** Cover each stage in order: Requirements → Planning → Design → Development → Testing → Deployment → Maintenance.

---

## 19. What is Agile methodology?

**Keywords:** `small iterations` · `collaboration` · `feedback` · `adapt` · `deliver value`

**Expectations:** Explain that Agile delivers value in small iterations through team collaboration, continuous feedback, and adapting to change.
